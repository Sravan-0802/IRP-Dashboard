---
name: IRP NOC form requirement
description: Mandatory internship NOC requirement for IRP students.
---

Every IRP student, whichever track they are on, must submit the No Objection Certificate (NOC) form for internships. Without submission, their profile cannot be taken forward for internship opportunities.

**Why:** The user provided this as a required dashboard notice.

**How to apply:** Keep the NOC form accessible to all IRP students, independent of journey level or track.
