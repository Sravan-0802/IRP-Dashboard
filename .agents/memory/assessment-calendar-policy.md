---
name: Assessment calendar policy
description: User requirements for where IRP assessment schedules are managed and accessed
---

IRP monthly assessment schedules must be admin-managed through a single-file upload, with subsequent manual editing, and accessed by students exclusively through Assessment Calendar. Each assessment has a name, date, and Online/Offline mode; its link is optional. Multiple assessments on the same date must remain independently accessible. Keep provided URLs in the admin editor, never print raw URL text to students, and make linked assessments open their saved destination.

Do not reintroduce automatically displayed scheduled assessment cards on the dashboard or individual track sections. Preserve results, practice, registration and project/Loom submissions.

**Why:** The user's monthly assessment calendar BRD requires a single point of access for schedules without affecting other IRP features; on 2026-11-13 the user clarified that links should be optional and not shown as raw text to students.

**How to apply:** Keep future schedule entry points directed to Assessment Calendar. Distinguish completed results and journey progress from scheduled assessment access when changing track pages.
