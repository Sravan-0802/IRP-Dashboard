---
name: Assessment calendar policy
description: User requirements for where IRP assessment schedules are managed and accessed
---

IRP monthly assessment schedules must be admin-managed through a single-file upload, with subsequent manual editing, and accessed by students exclusively through Assessment Calendar. Each assessment has a name, date, and Online/Offline mode; its link is optional. Multiple assessments on the same date must remain independently accessible. Keep provided URLs in the admin editor, never print raw URL text to students, and make linked assessments open their saved destination.

Keep general monthly schedules in Assessment Calendar. Exception: roster-assigned GenAI mock and main assessment links must also appear in both Assessment Hub and the GenAI dashboard. Preserve results, practice, registration and project/Loom submissions.

**Why:** The user's monthly assessment calendar BRD requires a single point of access for general schedules without affecting other IRP features; on 2026-11-13 the user clarified that links should be optional and not shown as raw text to students. They later explicitly requested assigned GenAI assessments in both Assessment Hub and the GenAI dashboard after uploaded batches were invisible there.

**How to apply:** Keep general calendar content separate from targeted access grants. Show assigned GenAI mock/main cards on both requested pages, retain roster scoping, show upcoming metadata without exposing the assessment URL before opening, and hide expired access. Do not duplicate general monthly events into track cards.
