---
name: Assessment calendar policy
description: User requirements for where IRP assessment schedules are managed and accessed
---

IRP monthly assessment schedules must be admin-managed through a single-file upload, with subsequent manual editing, and accessed by students exclusively through Assessment Calendar. Each assessment has a name, date, and Online/Offline mode; its link is optional. Multiple assessments on the same date must remain independently accessible. Keep provided URLs in the admin editor, never print raw URL text to students, and make linked assessments open their saved destination.

Keep admin-managed monthly calendar entries in Assessment Calendar; do not duplicate them into Assessment Hub. Assessment Hub is the common place for all roster-assigned assessment grants across MERN and GenAI. Show MERN grants on both the MERN dashboard and Assessment Hub, and GenAI grants on both the GenAI dashboard and Assessment Hub. Preserve results, practice, registration and project/Loom submissions.

**Why:** The user's monthly assessment calendar BRD requires a single point of access for general schedules without affecting other IRP features; on 2026-11-13 the user clarified that links should be optional and not shown as raw text to students. On 2026-11-23 they clarified that all assigned assessment grants belong in the common Assessment Hub, MERN grants also belong on the MERN dashboard, GenAI grants also belong on the GenAI dashboard, and calendar entries stay out of the Hub.

**How to apply:** Keep general calendar content separate from targeted access grants. Show roster-scoped assessment grants for each track in Assessment Hub and on that track's dashboard; show upcoming metadata without exposing the assessment URL before opening, and hide expired access. Do not copy calendar events into the Hub or track dashboards.
