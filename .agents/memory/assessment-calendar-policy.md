---
name: Assessment calendar policy
description: User requirements for where IRP assessment schedules are managed and accessed
---

IRP monthly assessment schedules must be admin-managed through a single-file upload, with subsequent manual editing, and accessed by students exclusively through Assessment Calendar. Each assessment has a name, date, Online/Offline mode and its own link. Multiple assessments on the same date must remain independently accessible.

Do not reintroduce automatically displayed scheduled assessment cards on the dashboard or individual track sections. Preserve results, practice, registration and project/Loom submissions.

**Why:** The user's monthly assessment calendar BRD requires a single point of access for schedules without affecting other IRP features.

**How to apply:** Keep future schedule entry points directed to Assessment Calendar. Distinguish completed results and journey progress from scheduled assessment access when changing track pages.
